# Worde catalog editorial policy

Catalog revision: `lexinexo-1.0.0-scowl-rel-2026.02.25-bilingual-hints`

## Lexical selection

- Candidate answers come only from the pinned SCOWL/ESDB dictionaries for
  `en_US`, `en_GB-ise`, and `en_GB-ize`.
- Answers use common American spelling. British variants are accepted only as
  guesses through the merged dictionary.
- Every answer is a lowercase English headword with 3–8 ASCII letters.
- Proper names, abbreviations, offensive words, artificial inflections, and
  overly specialized terms are excluded.
- The 1,000 answers are unique and follow the fixed length distribution. Within
  each length band, the order moves broadly from familiar/concrete concepts to
  less immediate or more abstract concepts.

## PT-BR and English content

- Each translation chooses one ordinary Brazilian-Portuguese equivalent for the
  selected sense.
- Each meaning is a complete, natural sentence written for this catalog. It
  explains only that selected sense and does not end in truncation marks.
- Each PT-BR clue is separately authored, complete, and specific. It may describe a
  situation, function, property, or association, but must not contain the answer
  or its selected translation as a standalone term.
- Every hinted level also has an individually authored `en-US` clue aligned to
  the exact same sense and word class. The English clue is a complete natural
  sentence and cannot contain the answer as a standalone word.
- English authoring and review are stored in `english_hints.json`. Every record
  has a canonical SHA-256 and separate authoring and independent-review approval.
- Generic prefix-plus-definition templates are prohibited. Meanings and clues
  must also be unique across the two modes.

## Single-sense semantic review

An additional semantic pass inspects all 1,000 meanings individually. The 621
texts that originally contained the standalone conjunction `ou` were classified:
lexical sense bifurcations were narrowed to one sense consistent with the PT-BR
translation and word class, while coordinations inside that same sense were
retained. The per-record result is stored in `single_sense_review.json`. This
pass was performed by OpenAI Codex and is not presented as human review.

## Two-pass agent review

Pass 1 authors and normalizes every answer, translation, meaning, and PT-BR or
English clue. Pass 2 is a separate procedural review of every resulting record
for sense and word-class alignment, natural PT-BR and English, circularity, clue
leakage and similarity, mechanical families, duplication, prohibited content,
and truncation. Both passes in revision 1.0.0 were performed with OpenAI Codex;
no human editorial review is claimed.

`editorial_review.json` binds both approvals to a SHA-256 hash of each canonical
catalog record. Any subsequent content edit invalidates the approval until the
review artifact is regenerated by an explicit two-pass review.
